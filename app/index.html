JozzyPay

Stablecoin payment links for small businesses, on the QDay Aevum testnet. A shop creates an invoice in tUSD8; a customer pays it; the money sits in escrow until the shop withdraws it. If the shop never withdraws and the invoice expires, the customer can refund themselves. One invoice can never exceed the spend cap (10,000 tUSD8).

Not connected. Use MetaMask (or any EVM wallet) on desktop.

How it works: createInvoice opens a payment request. The customer approves tUSD8 once, then payInvoice moves it into escrow. The merchant calls withdraw to collect. If the invoice expires unpaid, it cannot be paid. If it was paid and the merchant never withdrew, the payer can call refund after expiry. The merchant can cancel an unpaid invoice. Contract source is verified on the Aevum explorer. Test tokens only — tUSD8 and tQDAY have no real value.
